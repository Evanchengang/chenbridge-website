# Manufacturing Coordination

After supplier selection and order confirmation, ChenBridge manages the communication and execution process between customers and manufacturers.

Our goal is to ensure production progresses according to agreed requirements, timelines, and quality expectations.

## Our Process Includes

### Production Planning

We coordinate:

- Production schedule confirmation
- Material preparation
- Manufacturing milestones
- Delivery planning


### Progress Monitoring

We track:

- Production status
- Key project milestones
- Potential delays
- Supplier commitments


### Communication Management

ChenBridge acts as the communication bridge between customers and suppliers.

We support:

- Technical clarification
- Specification confirmation
- Change management
- Problem resolution


### Risk Control

We identify and manage:

- Production delays
- Material issues
- Manufacturing challenges
- Delivery risks


## Complete Visibility During Manufacturing

For overseas companies without a local team in China, ChenBridge provides real-time communication and execution support throughout the manufacturing process.

Customers gain confidence knowing their projects are actively managed locally.
