# Quality Inspection

Quality control is a critical part of successful international sourcing.

ChenBridge provides inspection support to ensure products meet customer specifications, quality standards, and delivery expectations before shipment.

## Our Inspection Process

### Pre-Production Review

Before production begins, we verify:

- Product specifications
- Technical requirements
- Material requirements
- Manufacturing plan


### In-Process Inspection

During production, we monitor:

- Production progress
- Manufacturing consistency
- Critical quality points
- Potential issues


### Final Inspection

Before shipment, we verify:

- Product appearance
- Dimensions and specifications
- Packaging condition
- Quantity confirmation
- Customer requirements


### Quality Issue Management

If issues occur, ChenBridge coordinates:

- Root cause analysis
- Supplier corrective actions
- Improvement plans
- Customer communication


## Quality Assurance Without a Local Office

Managing suppliers remotely can create quality risks.

ChenBridge provides local inspection coordination and supplier communication to help overseas customers maintain quality control throughout the manufacturing process.
