# Requirement Analysis

Every successful sourcing project starts with a clear understanding of customer requirements.

ChenBridge works with customers to analyze product specifications, application requirements, quality expectations, and delivery goals before engaging suppliers.

## Our Process Includes

### Requirement Understanding

We review:

- Product specifications
- Technical requirements
- Application environment
- Packaging requirements
- Quality standards


### Project Evaluation

We analyze:

- Manufacturing feasibility
- Material requirements
- Production complexity
- Expected timeline


### Sourcing Strategy Development

Based on customer needs, ChenBridge develops:

- Suitable supplier categories
- Manufacturing approach
- Cost evaluation strategy
- Quality control plan


## Why This Step Matters

Many sourcing challenges happen because requirements are unclear at the beginning.

ChenBridge helps customers define the right approach before supplier engagement, reducing communication gaps and improving project success.
