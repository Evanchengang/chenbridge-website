# RFQ & Quotation

ChenBridge provides a structured quotation process to help customers understand costs, evaluate options, and make informed sourcing decisions.

Our quotations are based on supplier input, material requirements, manufacturing processes, quality expectations, and delivery requirements.

## Our Quotation Process

### Requirement Review

Before quotation, we analyze:

- Product specifications
- Drawings and technical requirements
- Materials and components
- Packaging requirements
- Quality standards


### Supplier RFQ Management

We coordinate with qualified suppliers to collect:

- Manufacturing costs
- Material costs
- Production lead times
- Minimum order requirements
- Packaging and logistics information


### Cost Analysis

Our evaluation includes:

- Raw material costs
- Labor and manufacturing costs
- Packaging costs
- Tooling or setup costs
- Quality requirements
- Transportation considerations


### Quote Comparison & Recommendation

We provide customers with:

- Supplier comparison
- Cost analysis
- Manufacturing recommendations
- Risk considerations


## Transparent Pricing Approach

ChenBridge focuses on providing clear cost visibility rather than simply forwarding supplier quotations.

Customers receive a better understanding of:

- Where costs come from
- Available alternatives
- Potential cost-saving opportunities
- Recommended sourcing solutions
