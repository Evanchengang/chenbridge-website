# Supplier Selection

Finding the right manufacturing partner is one of the most important steps in building a reliable supply chain.

ChenBridge uses industry experience and supplier evaluation methods to identify manufacturers that match customer requirements.

## Our Process Includes

### Supplier Research

We identify potential suppliers based on:

- Product category
- Manufacturing capability
- Industry experience
- Customer requirements


### Supplier Evaluation

We evaluate:

- Factory capabilities
- Production equipment
- Quality systems
- Previous customer experience
- Export experience


### Capability Verification

Before cooperation, we review:

- Manufacturing processes
- Product samples
- Technical capability
- Production capacity


## Building Reliable Supplier Networks

ChenBridge does not simply find the lowest-cost supplier.

We focus on developing reliable manufacturing partners that can provide consistent quality, stable production, and long-term cooperation.
