# Logistics Coordination

ChenBridge supports overseas customers with export logistics coordination to ensure products move smoothly from Chinese manufacturers to global destinations.

We work with suppliers, freight partners, and customers to coordinate shipment preparation, documentation, and delivery execution.

## Our Services Include

### Shipment Preparation

We coordinate:

- Production completion confirmation
- Packaging verification
- Shipping schedule planning
- Export readiness checks


### Export Coordination

Support includes:

- Supplier communication
- Freight coordination
- Shipping document review
- Delivery timeline tracking


### International Transportation Support

We help manage:

- Ocean freight coordination
- Air shipment arrangements
- Container planning
- Delivery updates


### Delivery Visibility

Customers receive:

- Shipment status updates
- Communication support
- Issue escalation
- Delivery coordination


## End-to-End Supply Chain Support

From supplier selection to final delivery, ChenBridge provides local operational support to help overseas companies manage their China supply chain with confidence.

Our role is to ensure smooth communication, reliable execution, and transparent delivery throughout the entire process.
