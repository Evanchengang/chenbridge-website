# Manufacturing Coordination

ChenBridge helps overseas companies manage manufacturing execution in China by acting as the communication bridge between customers and suppliers.

We coordinate production activities, monitor progress, identify potential risks, and ensure suppliers meet agreed requirements and delivery schedules.

## Our Services Include

### Production Schedule Tracking

We monitor:

- Production milestones
- Manufacturing progress
- Material preparation
- Completion timelines


### Supplier Communication

We coordinate between customers and manufacturers regarding:

- Technical requirements
- Production updates
- Change requests
- Problem resolution


### Manufacturing Follow-Up

Support includes:

- Order status updates
- Production issue escalation
- Corrective action coordination
- Delivery preparation


### Risk Management

We help identify and address:

- Production delays
- Material shortages
- Quality risks
- Supplier execution issues


## Transparent Manufacturing Management

For companies without a local team in China, ChenBridge provides visibility and control throughout the manufacturing process.

Our goal is to ensure customers have clear communication, reliable execution, and confidence in their China supply chain.
