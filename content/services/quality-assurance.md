# Quality Assurance

ChenBridge provides quality assurance support for overseas companies working with Chinese manufacturers.

We help customers establish quality control processes, monitor supplier performance, and ensure products meet agreed specifications before shipment.

## Our Services Include

### Supplier Quality Evaluation

We assess:

- Supplier quality systems
- Manufacturing processes
- Quality control capabilities
- Previous production experience


### Inspection Coordination

We support:

- Incoming material inspection
- Production inspection
- Final inspection
- Pre-shipment verification


### Quality Issue Management

We coordinate:

- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions
- Supplier improvement plans
- Customer feedback communication


### Factory Audit Support

Our support includes:

- Factory capability evaluation
- Production process review
- Quality system assessment
- Continuous improvement recommendations


## Quality Control Without a Local Office

Many overseas companies face challenges managing quality when production is located in China.

ChenBridge provides local quality oversight and supplier communication to reduce risks and improve supply chain reliability.
