# Supplier Development

ChenBridge helps global companies identify and develop reliable manufacturing partners in China.

We provide local expertise to evaluate suppliers, verify capabilities, and establish stable long-term supply relationships.

## Our Services Include

### Supplier Identification

- Search qualified manufacturers
- Evaluate industry experience
- Match supplier capabilities with customer requirements


### Factory Evaluation

We assess:

- Production capability
- Manufacturing processes
- Quality systems
- Equipment and facilities


### Supplier Qualification

Support includes:

- Capability verification
- Sample development
- Communication coordination
- Supplier onboarding


## Why It Matters

Finding a supplier in China is easy.

Finding the right supplier that can consistently deliver quality products requires local experience and professional evaluation.

ChenBridge helps overseas companies reduce supplier risks and build reliable China-based supply chains.
