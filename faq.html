Frequently Asked Questions

Working with ChenBridge — your independent packaging sourcing representative in China.

What is ChenBridge?

ChenBridge is an independent packaging sourcing representative based in Suzhou, China. We help global companies source packaging materials, products, and complete packaging solutions from China — with local coordination, supplier development, and production follow-up.

What services does ChenBridge provide?

Our core services are supplier development (finding and qualifying packaging manufacturers), procurement support (RFQs, order management), production coordination (on-the-ground follow-up), and pre-shipment checks — so specifications stay aligned and progress stays visible.

What packaging categories do you cover?

Industrial and protective packaging, paper and corrugated packaging, custom and retail packaging, and multi-component packaging programs — including heavy-duty crates, export packaging, and pallets.

How is ChenBridge different from Alibaba or other sourcing platforms?

Online platforms solve the first step: identifying manufacturers. The greater challenge is what follows — making sure specifications are understood correctly, production remains visible, and responsibility does not disappear between emails. ChenBridge provides the local coordination that platforms don't: direct communication, production follow-up, and clear accountability.

How do you vet and qualify suppliers?

We evaluate suppliers against your requirements before any order is placed: capability assessment, sample and specification review, and structured RFQs. Only suppliers that fit your product and volume move forward.

Do you handle quality control?

Yes. Production follow-up and pre-shipment checks are part of our coordination service. Issues are surfaced early — while they are still inexpensive to correct — rather than discovered after goods are packed.

Why Suzhou / the Yangtze River Delta?

The Yangtze River Delta has one of the highest concentrations of packaging manufacturers in China. Being based in Suzhou puts us within reach of suppliers across the region for factory visits, production follow-up, and issue resolution.

What are your fees?

Our fee structure depends on the scope of the project — sourcing-only support, ongoing procurement coordination, or full production management. Contact us with your requirements and we will provide a clear proposal.

What are typical MOQs and lead times?

Minimum order quantities and lead times vary by packaging type, material, and customization level. As part of supplier development, we confirm realistic MOQs and timelines with qualified manufacturers before you commit.

How do we start working with ChenBridge?

Reach out via our contact page with a description of what you need to source. We will discuss your requirements, propose qualified supplier options, and agree on the scope of coordination before anything moves forward.
