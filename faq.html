About ChenBridge

ChenBridge is an independent packaging sourcing representative based in Suzhou, China. We help global companies source packaging materials, products, and complete packaging solutions from China — with local coordination, supplier development, and production follow-up.

The Yangtze River Delta has one of the highest concentrations of packaging manufacturers in China. Being based in Suzhou puts us within reach of suppliers across the region for factory visits, production follow-up, and issue resolution.

Services & Process

Our core services are supplier development (finding and qualifying packaging manufacturers), procurement support (RFQs, order management), production coordination (on-the-ground follow-up), and pre-shipment checks — so specifications stay aligned and progress stays visible.
