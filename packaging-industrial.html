Where Industrial Packaging Projects Go Wrong

Industrial packaging must survive handling, stacking, humidity, and weeks at sea. These are the problems we see most often — and the ones we work to prevent before your goods ship:

  • Crates arriving damaged or deformed at destination port
  • Incomplete ISPM-15 / fumigation documentation holding goods at customs
  • Insufficient internal blocking letting equipment shift in transit
  • Materials or board thickness not matching the approved sample

We help you work with manufacturers who understand export conditions — and keep specifications consistent from quotation to shipment.
