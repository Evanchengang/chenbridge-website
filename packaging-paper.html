Where Paper Packaging Projects Go Wrong

Paper packaging looks simple — until mass production starts. These are the issues we see most often, and the ones we help you control before they reach your customers:

  • Board weight or flute grade not matching the approved sample
  • Print color deviation and misregistration across runs
  • Dimensional tolerances affecting your packing line or carton fit
  • Inconsistent quality between batches

Beyond cartons, we also source kraft paper, edge protectors, honeycomb structures, and custom die-cut designs — matched to your protection requirements.
